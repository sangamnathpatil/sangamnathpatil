## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an **Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering graduate** passionate about **VLSI, digital circuit design, RTL development, and functional verification**. I enjoy converting digital logic concepts into synthesizable Verilog modules, writing testbenches, and debugging designs through simulation and waveform analysis.

- 🔭 **Built:** UART transmitter/receiver with top-level integration, synchronous FIFO, 4-road traffic-light FSM, and a 4-bit ALU.
- 🧠 **Strong interests:** RTL microarchitecture, finite-state machines, serial communication, and verification.
- 🌱 **Currently exploring:** SPI controller RTL, SystemVerilog, and verification methodologies.
- 🎯 **Next goals:** I2C, APB/AHB/AXI fundamentals, more structured verification, and SoC peripheral integration.
- 💼 **Career goal:** Entry-level **RTL Design / Design Verification / VLSI Design Engineer** roles.
- ⚡ **What I enjoy:** Designing hardware one module at a time and validating its behavior with testbenches.
